Getting started

  1. Open SwagMouse from Applications.
  2. Allow Input Monitoring when prompted. SwagMouse reads mouse reports and does not read keyboard input.
  3. Activate SwagMouse’s driver and approve it in System Settings → General → Login Items & Extensions.
  4. Choose your pointer speed and Enhance pointer precision setting. Start at 6/11 and size 1×.
  5. Enable SwagMouse, keep the pointer away from screen edges, and hold the mouse still briefly while it calibrates.

Pausing and removing SwagMouse

Use the menu bar to pause and resume. Quitting releases mouse capture. To remove the driver, choose Deactivate SwagMouse Driver from its menu before deleting the app.

Other input utilities and games

Karabiner can remain open for keyboard remapping. Another utility that captures or remaps the same physical mouse can conflict with SwagMouse. Games that read raw mouse input may receive adjusted virtual mouse reports. Trackpads retain their normal behavior. Some receiver media controls may be unavailable while mouse capture is enabled.

Troubleshooting

If movement is unavailable or calibration is interrupted, pause SwagMouse, move the pointer away from display edges, and resume while holding the mouse still. If capture fails, check whether another mouse utility owns the device.

You can open local diagnostics from Privacy & Support in SwagMouse’s menu. These files are not uploaded automatically; review them before sharing with support.
