SwagMouse Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

SwagMouse is a macOS utility that applies configurable speed and acceleration to connected mice. It processes mouse movement, buttons and scrolling on your Mac. It does not read keyboard input or record typed text.

Data Collection and Network Access

SwagMouse does not transmit input events, collect personal information, track you, or use advertising or analytics services. It has no account or network service.

Data Stored on Your Mac

Your pointer settings and selected mouse are stored locally. Local diagnostics contain mouse product names, device selection identifiers, report counts, timing statistics and driver status. They do not contain mouse coordinates, keyboard events or a movement history. Diagnostics are not uploaded automatically. If you choose to send them to support, review their contents first.

Permissions and Controls

Input Monitoring permission lets SwagMouse read mouse reports. macOS separately asks you to approve the bundled virtual mouse driver that delivers adjusted mouse input. Pause or quit SwagMouse to release mouse capture. You can revoke Input Monitoring in System Settings and deactivate the driver from SwagMouse’s menu.

Removing Local Data

You can delete files in SwagMouse’s diagnostics folder. To remove the app, deactivate its driver and delete SwagMouse. macOS may retain local preferences and logs until you delete them.

Children’s Privacy and Policy Changes

SwagMouse does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If its privacy practices change, this policy will be updated here.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, email jazappstudio@gmail.com. See SwagMouse support for setup and troubleshooting.
